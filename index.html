Travel Destinations Where have I been?

Copenhagen

Copenhagen is a beautiful city in Denmark known for its colorful buildings, canals, and historic architecture.

Why I Chose Copenhagen

I chose Copenhagen because my family lives there, which makes the city very special to me. I also enjoy its beautiful scenery, colorful buildings, and interesting history.

Things to See

Some popular places to see include Nyhavn, Tivoli Gardens, and the colorful waterfront.

Japan

Japan is one of my favorite travel destinations because of its food, culture, beautiful scenery, and exciting cities.

Why I Chose Japan

I chose Japan because I am interested in the culture, food, beautiful scenery, and unique places to visit. I would also like to experience the busy cities and traditional temples.

Things to Do in Japan

  • Visit Tokyo
  • See Mount Fuji
  • Explore temples and shrines
  • Try sushi and ramen
  • See the cherry blossoms

Mexico

Mexico is a beautiful place known for its delicious food, colorful culture, beautiful beaches, and historic landmarks.

Why I Chose Mexico

I chose Mexico because I would love to experience the culture, try authentic Mexican food, visit the beaches, and learn more about its history.

Things to Do in Mexico

  • Visit the beautiful beaches
  • Explore ancient Mayan ruins
  • Try tacos and other Mexican foods
  • Visit Mexico City
  • Experience local festivals and culture
  • See the historic landmarks
